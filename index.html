How to explain this project

Core analogy: A photo records the outside of a moment. Brain-State Circuit Replay asks whether we can also reconstruct the inside of a moment: perception, emotion, body sensation, attention, and meaning.

Thesis: We may not go back in time, but we may learn how to revisit brain-body states.

  1. Record: Photos, audio, smell, and context preserve external cues from a past moment.
  2. Infer: A target state is modeled as a distributed brain-body signature.
  3. Replay: Cueing, feedback, or stimulation nudges the current state toward that signature.
  4. Safeguard: Risk metrics prevent uncontrolled emotional replay or false certainty.
