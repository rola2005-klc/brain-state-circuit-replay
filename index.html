How to explain this project

Thesis: We may not go back in time, but we may learn how to revisit brain-body states.

  1. Encode: A meaningful state leaves a distributed neural signature.
  2. Decode: A future BCI estimates the current state and the target pattern.
  3. Replay: Closed-loop stimulation guides the brain toward the target.
  4. Safeguard: Risk metrics prevent uncontrolled emotional replay.
