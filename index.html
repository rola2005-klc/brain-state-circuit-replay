Project 012026 – ongoingConcept + toy simulation

Brain-State Circuit Resonance

“Photos record what we saw. Can we reconstruct what we were?”

A research thesis on whether future neurotechnology could help someone partially re-approach the inner state of a meaningful moment — safely, and without pretending to replay it.

  • Framed the Unreturnable Present Paradox and an integrated self-state schema
  • Built a 3D concept map and a closed-loop toy simulator
  • Stress-tested it: decoder accuracy under noise, stimulation precision vs. coverage
