Scientific boundaries

  • Concept map / simulation scaffold, not a brain-derived output, medical product, or literal time travel.
  • Not exact human memory replay and not exact visual playback.
  • EEG measures broad scalp-level correlates; it cannot read synaptic traces, identify engrams, or produce exact visual memory playback.
  • Synthetic classifier results illustrate pipeline behavior only; they are not empirical validation.
  • Brain visualization is conceptual; regions are not anatomically specific.
